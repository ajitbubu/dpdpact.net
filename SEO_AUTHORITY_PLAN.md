# DPDP Academy authority and distribution plan

This playbook covers work that cannot be completed by publishing pages alone. It deliberately avoids paid links, bulk directory submissions, synthetic reviews and invented professional credentials.

## 1. Indexation baseline

Owner action required:

1. Verify `https://dpdpact.net` as a Google Search Console domain property.
2. Submit `https://dpdpact.net/sitemap.xml`.
3. Inspect and request indexing for:
   - `/dpdp-compliance-deadline`
   - `/dpdp-compliance-templates`
   - `/dpdp-rules-2025`
   - `/dpdp-compliance-checklist`
   - `/blog/dpdp-consent-notice-guide`
   - `/blog/dpdp-breach-notification-guide`
   - `/implementation/healthcare`
4. Record indexed, discovered, crawled-not-indexed and canonical states weekly for eight weeks.

Do not repeatedly request indexing for an unchanged URL. Fix the reason a page is not being selected before resubmitting it.

## 2. Entity consistency

Use the same public facts everywhere:

- Name: DPDP Academy
- Canonical URL: `https://dpdpact.net`
- Description: Independent educational resource for the DPDP Act, 2023 and notified Rules, 2025.
- Public methodology: `https://dpdpact.net/editorial-policy`
- Official-source register: `https://dpdpact.net/sources`
- Public repository: `https://github.com/ajitbubu/dpdpact.net`

Do not publish a personal author or reviewer name until the person has approved the biography and relevant credentials.

## 3. Link-worthy assets to distribute

| Asset | Audience | Useful pitch |
|---|---|---|
| Compliance deadline timeline | DPO and legal newsletters | Three commencement phases tied to official notification |
| Open commencement CSV | Researchers and journalists | Reusable dates with source and last-checked field |
| Consent notice Markdown + CSV | Product, design and privacy engineering | Versioned purpose and withdrawal evidence, not generic policy prose |
| Breach workbook + CSV | Security and incident-response teams | Both Board stages and affected-person communication in one working file |
| Section-by-section Act reader | Teachers, law students and policy researchers | Searchable statutory text with stable section URLs |
| Industry implementation guides | Sector associations | Provisions and Schedules that genuinely change the sector analysis |

## 4. Outreach sequence

Prioritise relevance over volume:

1. Indian privacy and DPO professional groups.
2. Law-school privacy centres and legal research libraries.
3. Cybersecurity and incident-response communities.
4. SaaS, healthcare, fintech, edtech and startup associations for the matching industry guide.
5. Journalists and newsletter authors covering MeitY, privacy or digital regulation.
6. Organisations already maintaining DPDP resource lists.

Each message should point to one asset that improves an existing resource. Do not ask for a generic homepage link.

## 5. Suggested launch notes

### Deadline asset

DPDP Academy has published a source-linked timeline separating the provisions already in force from the Consent Manager phase on 13 November 2026 and the core operational phase on 13 May 2027. The dates are also available as an open CSV for researchers and compliance teams.

### Template asset

DPDP Academy has released editable, no-sign-up starter files for consent evidence and personal-data-breach response. The files use open Markdown and CSV formats so teams can inspect every field before adapting them.

## 6. Measurement log

Track monthly:

- Search Console impressions, clicks and average position by landing page and query.
- Indexed URLs and exclusion reasons.
- New referring domains and the exact page linked.
- Downloads of the four template files and commencement CSV.
- Brand-query impressions for “DPDP Academy” and `dpdpact.net`.
- Newsletter, academic, association and media citations.

Record the source, destination URL, date first seen and whether the citation is editorial. Never treat a self-created profile link as equivalent to an independent citation.

Operational files:

- `SEO_MEASUREMENT_LOG.csv` contains the eight-week baseline and weekly reporting schedule.
- `SEO_OUTREACH_PIPELINE.csv` records verified contact sources, the single relevant asset for each audience and the next action.
